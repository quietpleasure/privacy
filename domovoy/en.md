# Domovoy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Domovoy (the "app") helps you keep up with home maintenance: it reminds you when it is time to
replace a water filter, clean a water heater or an air conditioner, or submit meter readings,
and keeps a history of the work done. The app is developed by Andrei Sorokin ("we", "us").
Contact: pentod.android@gmail.com.

In short: everything you enter in the app stays on your phone. We have no servers, we do not
receive your records or photos, we show no ads and use no analytics. A third-party service
receives only information about your Premium subscription purchase.

## Data the app processes

### Items, tasks and history

The app stores:

- **items** — appliances and equipment you look after: name, type, where it is, model or
  serial number, notes and a photo;
- **tasks** — what needs to be done and how often, the next and last done dates;
- **history** — when a task was done, who did it (you or a specialist), cost and currency,
  notes.

This data is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. We do not receive
it and have no access to it. The app does not ask for your address; the notes and "Where"
fields contain only what you choose to enter.

### Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo of an appliance, its label,
its manual or a meter reading. Photos from your gallery are picked through the system picker:
the app receives only the photo you select, not access to your whole gallery. The photo is
resized and stored in the app's private storage.

### Reminders

Task reminders are created and stored on your device by Android and iOS system services.
No servers — ours or third-party — are involved. Notification permission is requested when
you first add tasks; you can revoke it in your phone settings.

### Backups

- **Backup file.** A backup file is created only when you ask for it and contains your items,
  tasks, history and photos. You choose where to save or send it (Google Drive, a messenger,
  email, a folder on your phone). We have no access to this file.
- **Android system backup.** If Google backup is enabled in your phone settings, Android may
  store the app's records (without photos) in your Google account. This is managed by Google
  under its privacy policy and can be turned off in your phone settings.

### Premium subscription

- **Payments** are processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never
  receive your card or payment details.
- **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.) verifies purchases and subscription status on our behalf.
  It receives: a random anonymous identifier generated by the app; your in-app purchase and
  subscription history; technical information (platform, app and OS version, device language
  and currency, and the IP address visible to any server you connect to). RevenueCat does
  not receive your name, email address, items, tasks, history or photos. RevenueCat's privacy
  policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## What the app does not do

- show ads or use advertising identifiers;
- use analytics or track your activity in the app or across other apps;
- access your location;
- sell or share data with third parties, except as described above for purchases.

## Retention and deletion

- An item is deleted together with all its tasks, history and photo when you delete it in
  the app. All data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- To delete your purchase information from RevenueCat, email us at pentod.android@gmail.com; we will delete
  it within 30 days. Payment history in Google Play and the App Store is kept under those
  stores' rules.

## Security

Purchase information is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by
the operating system: other apps cannot access it.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Significant changes
will be mentioned in the app's release notes. If the app gets features that need the internet
(for example, finding a specialist), we will describe here in advance what data they send.

## Contact

Questions about this policy and data deletion requests: pentod.android@gmail.com.
