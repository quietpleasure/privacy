# Warranty — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Warranty (the "app") helps you keep receipts and warranty periods for your purchases and
reminds you before a warranty ends. The app is developed by Andrei Sorokin ("we", "us").
Contact: pentod.android@gmail.com.

In short: everything you enter in the app stays on your phone. We have no servers, we do not
receive your items or receipt photos, we show no ads and use no analytics. A third-party
service receives only information about your Premium subscription purchase.

## Data the app processes

### Items and receipt photos

Item name, store, price and currency, purchase date, warranty period, notes and receipt
photos are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. We do not receive
this data and have no access to it.

### Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo of a receipt. Photos from
your gallery are picked through the system picker: the app receives only the photo you
select, not access to your whole gallery. The photo is resized and stored in the app's
private storage.

### Reminders

Warranty reminders are created and stored on your device by Android and iOS system
services. No servers — ours or third-party — are involved.

### Backups

- **Backup file.** A backup file is created only when you ask for it. You choose where to
  save or send it (Google Drive, a messenger, email, a folder on your phone). We have no
  access to this file.
- **Android system backup.** If Google backup is enabled in your phone settings, Android may
  store the app's records (without receipt photos) in your Google account. This is managed
  by Google under its privacy policy and can be turned off in your phone settings.

### Premium subscription

- **Payments** are processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never
  receive your card or payment details.
- **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.) verifies purchases and subscription status on our behalf.
  It receives: a random anonymous identifier generated by the app; your in-app purchase and
  subscription history; technical information (platform, app and OS version, device language
  and currency, and the IP address visible to any server you connect to). RevenueCat does
  not receive your name, email address, items or receipt photos. RevenueCat's privacy policy:
  https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## What the app does not do

- show ads or use advertising identifiers;
- use analytics or track your activity in the app or across other apps;
- access your location;
- sell or share data with third parties, except as described above for purchases.

## Retention and deletion

- Items and receipt photos are deleted when you delete an item in the app, clear the app's
  data, or uninstall the app.
- To delete your purchase information from RevenueCat, email us at pentod.android@gmail.com; we will delete
  it within 30 days. Payment history in Google Play and the App Store is kept under those
  stores' rules.

## Security

Purchase information is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by
the operating system: other apps cannot access it.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Significant changes
will be mentioned in the app's release notes.

## Contact

Questions about this policy and data deletion requests: pentod.android@gmail.com.
